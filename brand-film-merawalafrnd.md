# Brand Film — #MeraWalaFrnd

> **Format:** Hero brand film (60–90 sec anthem) + 20s cutdown
> **Positioning line:** *Sabka apna, mera wala frnd.*
> **The one truth (from FRND DNA):** FRND sells the relief of not being alone. The film must earn that feeling, not sell an app.
> **Structure the brief asked for:** open on the problems → turn → propose *mera wala frnd* as the solution.

---

## 1. THE INSIGHT

Everyone has friends. Nobody has *their* friend.

The group chat is loud but no one asks how you actually are. There are 400 followers and zero people to call at 2 AM. The city is full and the room is empty. Indian men are told to be "strong," so they go quiet — and quiet becomes alone.

FRND isn't "make new friends." FRND is finding the *one* — **mera wala frnd** — the person whose voice makes the night feel less long.

**Emotional job of the film:** name the loneliness out loud (nobody does), then hand over the relief.

---

## 2. THE ARC (Problem → Turn → Solution)

| Beat | Time | What happens | What the viewer feels |
|------|------|--------------|----------------------|
| **The Problems** | 0–35s | A montage of modern loneliness — surrounded but unseen | "That's me." |
| **The Turn** | 35–45s | One phone lights up. A real voice answers. | A crack of hope |
| **Mera Wala Frnd** | 45–75s | The relief — real conversations, real voices, night becomes lighter | "I want this." |
| **The Brand** | 75–90s | Logo, line, first call free | Clear next step |

---

## 3. THE FILM — SCENE BY SCENE

**Visual language:** warm amber and phone-glow blues. Faces we linger on. Real rooms, not sets. No couples (FRND is not a dating app — it's *friend*). The phone is a warm light source, never a cold screen.

---

### ACT I — THE PROBLEMS (0–35s)

Open on silence. City sounds fade under a soft piano.

**SCENE 1 — The loud table, the quiet person (0–8s)**
A group of friends laughing at a dhaba. Push in slowly on one guy at the edge — smiling, but his eyes are somewhere else. The laughter around him goes muffled.

> **VO (Hindi, warm, unhurried):**
> *"Sab ke beech mein bhi... kabhi kabhi akela lagta hai."*
> (Even surrounded by everyone... sometimes you feel alone.)

**SCENE 2 — 400 followers, zero calls (8–16s)**
Bedroom, night. A boy scrolls reels, thumb moving on autopilot, face lit blue. The notification count is high. His recent-calls list is empty. Clock on the wall: it's late.

> **VO:** *"Phone bhara hai... par baat karne wala koi nahi."*
> (The phone is full... but there's no one to actually talk to.)

**SCENE 3 — The "I'm fine" (16–24s)**
A video call with family. "Sab theek hai, mummy." He hangs up. The smile drops the second the screen goes dark. He sits in the quiet.

> **VO:** *"'Sab theek hai' bolte bolte... thak jaate hain."*
> (You get tired of saying "everything's fine.")

**SCENE 4 — 2 AM (24–35s)**
The evergreen FRND beat. Wall clock hits **2:00 AM**. He's awake, staring at the ceiling, phone face-down. He picks it up, opens the contacts, scrolls the whole list top to bottom — and puts it back down. No one to call.

> **VO (softer):** *"Aur raat ke 2 baje... jab sabse zyada baat karni hoti hai... tab hi koi nahi hota."*
> (And at 2 AM... when you most need to talk... that's exactly when there's no one.)

*Beat. Silence. Hold on his face.*

---

### ACT II — THE TURN (35–45s)

**SCENE 5 — One light comes on (35–45s)**
His thumb hovers. He opens **FRND**. A call connects. And for the first time in the film — **a real voice answers.**

> **GIRL'S VOICE (warm, unscripted-feeling, off-screen):**
> *"Aree, so nahi rahe? Kya hua... sunao."*
> (Hey, not sleeping? What happened... tell me.)

His face changes. The smallest exhale. The music lifts — piano gains a heartbeat.

> **VO:** *"Bas ek awaaz... aur raat chhoti ho jaati hai."*
> (Just one voice... and the night gets shorter.)

---

### ACT III — MERA WALA FRND (45–75s)

Now the montage inverts. Same people, same rooms — but lit warm, alive.

**SCENE 6 — The relief montage (45–68s)**
Quick, human moments, each a different person on FRND:
- The dhaba guy, now walking home, laughing into his earphones — *really* laughing.
- A boy on his terrace at night, gesturing wildly telling a story to a voice that laughs back.
- A girl on the other end, cross-legged on her bed, genuinely into the conversation.
- Someone playing a game while chatting, someone just quietly listening to another person vent — *"haan main sun rahi hoon, bol."*
- The 2 AM guy from Scene 4 — now smiling in the dark, phone warm against his ear.

> **VO (building):**
> *"Koi number maange bina. Koi judgement nahi. Koi dikhawa nahi."*
> (Without asking for a number. No judgement. No pretending.)
> *"Sirf ek insaan... jo sach mein sunta hai."*
> (Just one person... who actually listens.)

**SCENE 7 — Naming it (68–75s)**
Faces, one after another, each turning slightly toward camera. Text rises with the VO:

> **VO:** *"Sabka koi na koi hota hai. Bas... mil nahi paate."*
> (Everyone has their person. They just... haven't found them.)
>
> **VO (landing the line):** *"Ab mil jaayenge. #MeraWalaFrnd."*

---

### ACT IV — THE BRAND (75–90s)

**SCENE 8 — End card**
Hard cut (not fade) to the FRND hot-pink brand card.

- Logo: **FRND** (pink)
- Line: **Sabka apna. Mera wala frnd.**
- Offer: **Pehli call FREE** (FREE in yellow/gold)
- Both app-store badges

> **VO (final, warm):** *"FRND. Mera wala frnd. Pehli call bilkul free."*

---

## 5. TONE, MUSIC, CASTING

- **Tone:** Tender, honest, never preachy. It should feel like the film *saw* the viewer. Zero salesy energy until the end card.
- **Music:** Solo piano → adds a soft heartbeat/pulse at the Turn → warm strings + light percussion in Act III → resolves on one clean note at the logo.
- **Casting:** Real-looking, relatable young men (18–28), tier-1 to tier-3. Not models. The FRND voices should sound *warm and real*, never performed. **No couples, no romance framing** — this is friendship and being heard.
- **Language:** Hindi/Hinglish hero cut. Built to re-VO into Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam using the same picture (regional voices swapped per market).

---

## 6. WHY THIS WORKS (grounded in FRND winner DNA)

- **loneliness_cure + parasocial_intimacy** — the two mandatory triggers — carry the entire film.
- **The 2 AM beat** (Scene 4) is FRND's single most proven emotional anchor; the film builds *toward* it, then breaks it with a real voice.
- **"Koi number maange bina"** echoes the proven "no number needed" value prop.
- **Pehli call FREE** on the end card — the non-negotiable zero-friction CTA.
- **Hard cut to hot-pink card** matches the established brand grammar.
- **Not a couple, not dating** — protects the *friend* positioning that FRND owns.

---

## 7. CUTDOWNS

- **20s performance cut:** Scene 4 (2 AM) → Scene 5 (voice answers) → end card. Straight problem→solution for paid social.
- **6s bumper:** Black → "Raat ke 2 baje, koi nahi?" → voice answers → hot-pink card. For YouTube/pre-roll.
- **Static / billboard:** Phone glow on a face in the dark. Six words: **"Mera wala frnd. Pehli call free."**
