# 08 · The Habit System — a disciplined life that survives bad weeks

**Design principle:** you told me you keep stopping. So this system is built for the *stopping*, not for the perfect weeks. Discipline isn't intensity — it's re-starting fast, forever.

## The 8 daily lines (tracked in [habit-tracker.csv](habit-tracker.csv))

| # | Habit | Full version | Minimum-viable version (bad days) |
|---|-------|--------------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | Sleep | In bed 11 pm, up 6 am | Screens off by 11:30 |
| 2 | Move | Gym (M/W/F) or 8K steps | 15-min walk |
| 3 | Meditate | 15-min Future-Manasvi practice (file 04) | 10 slow breaths before phone |
| 4 | Content | 60–90 min block per weekly machine (file 02) | Write 3 hooks in Notes app |
| 5 | Learn | 20 min — month's career-calendar topic | Read one article from the month's source |
| 6 | Read | 25 pages (file 07) | 5 pages |
| 7 | Language | 20 min Spanish + 20 min English drills | 1 Duolingo lesson + 1 recorded sentence |
| 8 | Money | 2-min expense log + 2-line journal | Log expenses only |

**Scoring:** each line = 1 point; minimum-viable counts as a FULL point (this is the trick — the system rewards showing up, so you never face the "all or nothing" cliff that used to make you quit). 6+/day = green day. Target: 5 green days/week. NOT 7. Two imperfect days are budgeted *by design* — the plan expects your bad days, so bad days can't break the plan.

## The three laws

1. **Never miss twice.** Any habit at 0 yesterday gets its minimum-viable version done *first* today, before anything else.
2. **The 10-minute rule.** Resistance? Do 10 minutes, permission to stop after. (You'll finish 80% of the time; the other 20% still scored the point.)
3. **Environment beats willpower** (Atomic Habits, ch. 6 — your August read): gym bag packed at night by the door · phone charges *outside* arm's reach, alarm across the room · tripod stays standing in filming corner · book on the pillow every morning · junk food simply not in the house (you can't eat what isn't there) · Instagram logged out on laptop; posting via scheduler.

## The Sunday Review (30 min, the keystone — if you keep ONE ritual, keep this)

1. Fill the week's tracker row totals; compute green days.
2. Content metrics: reels shipped, hook rates, follows (file 02's Friday numbers).
3. Body: weekly weight average + workouts done.
4. Money: expenses vs plan, debt balance update (watching it drop is genuinely addictive).
5. Write 3 lines: *What worked? What broke? ONE change for next week?* (One. Not five. Changing five things is how plans die.)
6. Load next week: 10 content hooks listed, gym days in calendar, one thing to look forward to.

## Streak insurance (for the week life explodes — it will, ~3 times this year)

Deadline crunch / period from hell / family emergency / just done with everything → declare a **Maintenance Week** in the tracker (write "MW" in Notes column). Only 4 lines required: Sleep, minimum Move, minimum Meditate, Money log. Everything else legally paused, zero guilt, zero "I ruined it." Maximum 1 MW per month. A declared retreat is discipline; a silent collapse is the old pattern. This single rule is the difference between "I keep stopping" and "I paused twice and still shipped 250 reels."

## Quarterly (with the 90-day content sprints)

- Re-read your own README + this file (you wrote these goals; re-reading is re-signing).
- One full rest weekend. No content, no learning. Recovery is part of the program, not a betrayal of it.
- Re-take progress photos, re-run the numbers, adjust ONE system.
