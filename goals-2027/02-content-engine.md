# 02 · The Content Engine — Instagram + YouTube (goal: 5M) & LinkedIn (goal: 1M)

**First, the math you'd demand from any media plan** (you're a performance marketer — you'd never buy a campaign without it):

- 5M followers by mid-2027 means sustained non-follower reach of **tens of millions per month** for ~9 straight months. In India that has happened — to narration pages, comedy pages, transformation journeys — but it cannot be scheduled. It requires (a) a repeatable format people binge, (b) 300+ shipped attempts, (c) 2–3 breakout moments you *earn* by being present when they hit.
- **Ladder we commit to (inputs → checkpoints):** 1K by Sep 2026 → 10K by Nov 2026 → 50K by Jan 2027 → 250K by Apr 2027 → 1M by Jul 2027 (Strong) → 5M (Outlier: the format catches fire; we build for it, we don't budget on it).
- **LinkedIn:** fewer than ~20 people in India have 1M. 25–50K by mid-2027 = elite tier, gets you 100% of the career/brand value. That's the real target; 1M stays on the vision board.

**Non-negotiable input:** ~250 reels + ~150 LinkedIn posts over 12 months. That's the whole secret. Everything below just makes those units better.

## Your niche (decided, stop deliberating)

**"THE REBUILD" — a girl from Mirzapur rebuilds her whole life in public, narrated like a story.**

Why this wins for you specifically:
- Your proven asset is your **narration voice** (itsme_vishra reels, podcast episodes) + Hindi imagery writing (your course exercises are literally training this).
- You don't need a lifestyle you don't have — the *lack* is the story. "Boring" is relatable; relatable scales in India.

- Brand-safe and monetizable: fitness, beauty/hair, edtech, fintech, food brands all buy this audience.

### Three pillars (rotate; 70/20/10)
1. **Kahaani/Narration (70%)** — 45–90s narrated reels in your Hindi-imagery voice: micro-stories, observations, poetry, "Mirzapur memories," Bangalore-alone diaries. Mass-reach engine.
2. **The Climb (20%)** — documentation: weigh-ins, debt counter, job-hunt updates, renovation progress. Builds *parasocial investment* — these people will follow you for years.
3. **Ad-Brain (10%)** — "why this ad works" teardowns in Hinglish. Authority engine; feeds LinkedIn, brand deals, and job offers. (On LinkedIn this ratio flips: 70% Ad-Brain / 30% Climb.)

**One channel identity everywhere:** same handle, same face, same intro line. Suggest keeping `itsme_vishra` or renaming to something ownable — decide once in week 1, never again.

**Posting:** 6–9 pm IST weekdays; trending audio at low volume under narration; covers with 4–6 word curiosity text; first line of caption = second hook.

## 90-day sprint structure (repeat 4×)

- **Days 1–30:** ship volume, try 6 formats, judge nothing.
- **Days 31–60:** kill bottom 3 formats, double down on top 2, engineer one collab/duet per week.
- **Days 61–90:** series-ify the winner ("Part 7 of paying off my debt…" — series are how accounts explode), pitch 5 micro-brand collabs.
- **Metrics that matter (in order):** hook rate (>30% watch past 3s), average watch % (>70% on <60s), saves+shares, follows per 1K reach. Views are noise; these are signal.

## What I will not let you do

- Buy followers/engagement (brands check via HypeAuditor/Modash — fake numbers = zero brand money + throttled reach).
- Post inconsistently in week 3 because week 2 "flopped." 90-day sprints judge formats, not days.
- Copy trends outside your voice. Clone *structures*, never souls.
