## The reading system (Aug–Dec 2026)

10 books / 150 days = 2 books a month = **~25 pages a day**. That's 30–35 minutes. It's in the habit tracker as a daily line. Rules:

- Reading slot: 30 min before sleep (replaces the last scroll of the night — trade 1:1, no extra time needed) or audiobook during your 8K steps (Audible/Storytel — audio counts, snobs are wrong).
- Every book ends with a **1-reel review in Hindi** ("is kitaab ne mera dimaag khola…") — 10 free content pieces, and teaching = retention.
- Falling behind? Skim the middle-third of any business book guilt-free. Authors pad. Finish and move.
