## Step 0 — THIS WEEK: the debt protocol

You're in debt with ₹0 saved. Nothing in this file works until this is structured. One evening:

1. **List every debt** (amount, interest rate, EMI) in one sheet. Credit card first — Indian card interest is 36–42%/yr, it is a house fire.
2. **Avalanche:** minimums on everything; every spare rupee to the highest-interest debt. If a personal loan at ~11–14% can consolidate 40% card debt, do it (only through your bank, no app loans, ever).
3. **Freeze:** no new EMIs, no BNPL, no card swipes that can't be cleared same month. 12 months.
4. **Auto-split salary on payday (pay yourself first):** 55–60% living · 25–30% debt kill · 10% "future fund" (even ₹2K/month — the habit matters before the amount) · 5% guilt-free (poetry books, one nice coffee — a plan with zero joy gets abandoned).
5. Track in the money log habit (2 min/day, see habit tracker).

**Sequence, strictly:** debt zero → ₹1.5L emergency fund (2 months expenses, FD/liquid fund) → then renovation saving + index SIP (any Nifty 50 index fund, auto-debit). Read *Psychology of Money* + *Make Epic Money* from the reading list — that's your "financial mismanagement" education, 500 total pages.

## The ₹40L Mirzapur renovation — phased, not fantasized

A ₹40L lump by mid-2027 sits in the Strong/Outlier column. Homes, though, are renovated in phases everywhere in India — and a phased renovation is *better content* than a finished one (renovation/UP-home-transformation content is a massive genre — Pillar 2 gold: "Mirzapur wala ghar, episode 1").

| Phase | What | Cost | Funded by |
|-------|------|------|-----------|
| 0 — Q4 2026 | Detailed BOQ with a local contractor + structural priorities | ₹0–20K | Salary |
| 1 — Mid/late 2027 | Structural + waterproofing + bathrooms + kitchen (the "parents' comfort" core) | ₹8–12L | Base-scenario savings + freelance |
| 2 — 2028 | Flooring, façade, paint, furniture | ₹10–15L | New-role salary + creator income |
| 3 — 2028–29 | The beautiful extras | ₹10–15L | Strong/Outlier income |

**Rule: no loan against hoped-for creator income.** If Outlier hits early, collapse the phases. If Base, phase 1 still happens and your parents still get the comfortable core by end-2027. Either way Mirzapur sees progress — which is what the guilt actually wants.

## Bungalow + premium 8-seater by 2028 — the gates

₹1.5–3Cr of assets. Dr. Joe scene for the morning meditation: absolutely — you on that roof, keys in hand, family in the car. Purchase decision: gated by math, not by feeling:

- **Car gate:** buy the ₹30–60L 8-seater when its price ≤ 20% of liquid net worth *or* stable annual income ≥ 2× its price. Until then, rent an Innova for family trips — same memory, ₹8K, zero EMI chain.

- These gates aren't me shrinking your dream — they're me protecting it. The #1 killer of rising creators/professionals is fixed costs bought at peak optimism.
