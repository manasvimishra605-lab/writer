## The honest math

Your current comp is (from your own doc) below market and you're in debt. Nobody goes from that to a ₹70 LPA *salary* in 12 months through courses — that's not pessimism, it's how comp bands work. Here's what *is* real:

| Path | 12-month realistic comp | Who pays it |
|------|--------------------------|-------------|
| Creative Strategist / Content Lead, Indian consumer startup | ₹15–25 LPA | CRED, Zepto, Dream11, MPL, Meesho, fintechs |
| **UA Creative Strategist, global mobile-gaming studio (remote)** | **₹30–45 LPA ($35–55K)** | Kwalee, Tripledot, Homa, Voodoo, Playrix, SciPlay, Scopely |
| Senior UA / Creative Lead (18–30 months out) | ₹50–80 LPA ($60–90K) | Same studios, one level up |
| **Salary + freelance + creator income stacked** | **₹25–70L total in year one** | This is the honest route to your number |

**The thesis:** Your FRND work — performance creatives that cut CAC from ~600 to ~75, on gaming and social verticals — is *exactly* the job called **User Acquisition (UA) Creative Strategist** in mobile gaming. It's one of the few marketing roles where global studios hire remotely from India and pay international-ish salaries, because performance is measurable and language/timezone barely matter. You are not "changing careers." You are renaming what you already do and selling it to a global market. ₹70 LPA is the *second* jump on this ladder, not the first — but total income (salary + freelance + creator) can plausibly cross ₹40–70L in the Strong scenario by mid-2027. That's the honest version of your goal, and it's worth everything you'll put into it.
