# 06 · English to top band + one new language

## Part 1 — English: IELTS-top-band level (target Band 8.0)

**One honest check first:** IELTS the *exam* (₹18K) is only worth booking if a concrete application (visa, foreign university, an employer that asks) needs the certificate. IELTS the *skill level* — Band 8 English — is worth everything for you regardless: global remote interviews, international clients, and bilingual content all run on it. So: train to Band 8 now; book the exam in Feb 2027 only if a real application exists. Don't buy a certificate to frame.

**Your realistic arc:** you already work in English daily. Current level is likely 6.5–7.0 conversational. 7.5 is very achievable by early 2027; 8.0 is the stretch — the gap is precision (grammar slips, articles, spelling under time) and *structured* speaking/writing, not vocabulary.

### Daily drills (20 min, embedded in the habit tracker)
- **10 min speaking:** record yourself answering one IELTS Part 2 cue card ("describe a person who influenced you…") on your phone. Listen back. Cringe. Improve. This doubles as camera/voice training for content — same muscle, two goals.
- **10 min writing:** one paragraph in *formal register* (your casual register is already strong). Alternate: opinion paragraph / describe-a-chart. Get it critiqued by AI immediately — you have Claude; use it as a ruthless examiner: "Grade this as an IELTS examiner, band + 3 specific fixes."

### The 8-week sprint (Feb–Mar 2027, slotted in the career calendar)
| Weeks | Focus | Resource |
|-------|-------|----------|
| 1–2 | Format + diagnostics: one full mock, find your weak paper | Cambridge IELTS 17 & 18 (authentic past papers — the only practice books worth buying) |
| 3–4 | Writing Task 2 templates + Task 1 | IELTS Liz — https://ieltsliz.com (best free site, period) |
| 5–6 | Speaking fluency + Listening speed | IELTS Advantage — https://www.ieltsadvantage.com · E2 IELTS (YouTube) |
| 7 | Reading timing drills (20 min/passage, strict) | Cambridge books again |
| 8 | 2 full timed mocks + review | Free official prep: https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/prepare |

Band-8 killers to drill specifically: articles (a/the — Hindi speakers' #1 leak), subject-verb agreement under speed, spelling in Listening, and *answering the actual question* in Writing Task 2.

## Part 2 — The new language: **Spanish** (decided, with reasons)

Why Spanish over the alternatives for *your* goals:
- **Reach:** ~600M speakers; the second-biggest content market on Earth. A Hindi creator who can drop Spanish hooks/subtitles has a real 2028 expansion lane (your 5M math loves a second market).
- **Speed:** for an English speaker it's the fastest major language (phonetic spelling, familiar script) — you can hit conversational A2–B1 in a year at 20 min/day. Japanese/Korean (the gaming-romantic picks) cost 4× the hours for the same level; wrong trade while the career sprint is on.
- **Career:** LatAm is a huge mobile-gaming UA market — "creative strategist with Hindi + English + Spanish market sense" is a genuinely rare line on a resume.

### The stack (20 min/day)
| Tool | Use | Cost |
|------|-----|------|
| **Language Transfer — Complete Spanish** — https://www.languagetransfer.org | THE core. 90 audio lessons that teach you to *think* in Spanish. Do one lesson/day, start here. | Free |
| **Dreaming Spanish** (YouTube/site) — https://www.dreamingspanish.com | Comprehensible input videos; watch 1/day from month 2 | Free tier |
| Duolingo | 5-min daily streak glue (it's a habit anchor, not a teacher) | Free |
| SpanishDict — https://www.spanishdict.com | Dictionary + conjugation drills | Free |
| italki — https://www.italki.com | 1 conversation lesson/week from month 4 (~₹600–900/lesson) | Paid, later |

### Milestones
- **Oct 2026:** Language Transfer done once; can introduce yourself, order, ask.
- **Jan 2027:** A2 — survive a slow conversation; understand Dreaming Spanish intermediate.
- **Jul 2027:** B1 conversational — hold a 15-min conversation with your italki tutor; post your first reel with Spanish subtitles (content moment: "UP girl speaks Spanish" is a hook that writes itself).

**Sequencing note (protecting you from yourself):** Spanish is 20 min/day *max* until the job switch lands. The IELTS sprint (Feb–Mar) pauses Spanish entirely. One language sprint at a time; the tracker enforces it.
